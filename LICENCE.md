## 🧩 About

**Ready or Not Trainer** is a curated list of Ready or Not open-source tools:

- **Mod Menus** — ESP, aimbot, no recoil, infinite health, speed hack
- **UI Automation Bots** — trigger bot, auto-shoot, input helpers for single-player study
- **Quality-of-Life Mods** — FOV changer, no flash, no suppression, overlay helpers
- **Research Frameworks** — training layouts and config notes for educational use

Based on publicly available community tools like **RoN Hack Menu**, **SWAT Mod Menu**, and **QOLMod**.

### 🎮 Core Hacks
- ESP — see enemies through walls with boxes, names, distances

- Speed Hack — adjust movement speed (0.1x to 5x)
- Infinite Health — god mode for single-player scenarios

### 🎨 Cosmetics & Unlocks
- All Weapons Unlocked — access every gun, attachment, and gear

- Trigger Bot — auto-shoot when crosshair is on enemy
- No Flash — ignore flashbang effects

- Infinite Ammo — no reload, unlimited magazines
- Freeze Enemies — stop enemy movement for training
